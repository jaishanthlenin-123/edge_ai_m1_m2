## 3. Recommended Photography Guidelines

1. **Lighting:** Use diffuse indoor lighting or desk lamp with no harsh reflections on the lens.
2. **Background:** Use a clean, non-cluttered background (e.g., solid white, grey, or cutting mat).
3. **Focus:** Ensure crisp focal focus on the enclosure geometry and mounting standoffs.
4. **Resolution:** Recommended $1920 \times 1080$ (1080p) or higher JPEG/PNG format.
5. **Placement:** Save photos to `evidence/M1/07_cad_design/photos/`.
